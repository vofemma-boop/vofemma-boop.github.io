Fair Sort – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Fair Sort is a puzzle game published by ComfyCool (Belgium). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information. There is no account and no sign-in. Your game progress (levels, stars, daily results, settings) is stored only on your own device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and app interaction and diagnostic data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy & ad choices, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

Fair Sort is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to object to processing. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, requests about ad data are best directed to Google; you can also contact us and we will help.
